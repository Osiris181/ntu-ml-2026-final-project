# E18 feature feasibility audit and frozen conventions

E18 adapts the feature families in §3.3 of Kottas (2026) to the project’s absolute intraday direction classifier. The paper describes 196 predictors and lists a formula catalogue in supplementary Table S1. The publisher served the supplement as an attachment whose contents were unavailable in this session. The individual E18 formulas are independent adaptations of the requested families. [Main article](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7099/14/9/393), [publisher supplement](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/economies14090393/s1).

The traded monetary amount field was available in the audited data. The Amihud proxy divides the absolute adjusted close-to-close return by this reported turnover, in the provider’s units. Average transaction size divides the same reported amount by transaction count. The dataset does not establish a cross-period currency adjustment or economic capacity interpretation for these proxies.

## Formula conventions

The [frozen catalogue](feature_catalogue.json) lists every requested candidate, its exact raw-column names, window, source scope, prior overlap, implementation status, and origin. `s` is the completed source session. `O,H,L,C` denote adjusted OHLC, `V` share volume, `A` traded amount, and `T` transaction count. `r=C/O−1`, `q=O/C_previous−1`, `u=C/C_previous−1`, and `m_n=C_s/C_(s−n)−1`. True range `TR` is `max(H−L, abs(H−C_previous), abs(L−C_previous))`; the new ATR divides its simple rolling mean by the latest adjusted close. Rolling maximum and minimum refer to closes. All volatility and dispersion calculations use population standard deviation. Semideviation gives zero contribution to nonnegative returns. A rolling window requires every constituent value. A lagged return or true range adds its prior close to the raw lookback.

Stock windows are computed within each stock on completed session rows, then joined once by the latest strictly earlier source date. The market aggregates use the fixed metadata Top100 universe and valid contributors on the latest earlier supplied market session. The dispersion is missing with zero contributors and zero for a singleton. Market rolling features use consecutive supplied market sessions and require a complete window of valid daily aggregates. Relative stock features compare the stock against peers on that stock’s source date. This keeps the comparison on one completed calendar session when a stock is absent from the latest market date. There is no peer imputation before aggregation. The stock and market source indices and dates, individual stock-session index, and per-market-date valid contributor counts are saved. These describe the agreed conservative date policy. Actual pre-09:00 publication times remain unverified.

The only exact F0 duplicate in the requested expansion is `relative_amount_20d`. It is excluded from the additions and retained in shared F0. E2 already tested the exact 10-session momentum and 5-session close-return volatility. E4 used arithmetic return means, while E18 uses compounding. The previously tested E3 intraday and 5-session momentum deviations agree with their E18 counterparts when peer source sessions align. E18 keeps these earlier experimental overlaps because they are absent from F0 and part of the predefined families. Candle body and the difference between close and open locations are algebraically related. All requested additions with data support remain in the frozen families regardless of redundancy diagnostics.

The historical Phase 1 recommendation rule was frozen before training. A candidate must exceed E3 on the equal-year mean, beat E3 in at least two years, and have a worst fixed four-month block at least as strong as E3’s. Eligible arms are ordered by historical mean score. The bootstrap and feature diagnostics qualify the interpretation, and Phase 1 data play no role in this decision. The fixed blocks within each validation year are January–April, May–August and September–December.
